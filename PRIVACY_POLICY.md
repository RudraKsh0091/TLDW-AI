# Privacy Policy for TLDW AI

Last updated: July 2026

TLDW AI is an AI-powered browser extension that allows users to ask questions and generate summaries for YouTube videos.

## Information Collected

The extension processes:

- The current YouTube video URL.
- The video's transcript.
- Questions entered by the user.

This information is used only to provide AI-powered question answering and summarization.

## Data Storage

Video transcripts may be temporarily or permanently stored on the backend to avoid repeated indexing and improve performance.

The extension does not collect:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Passwords
- Payment information
- Health information
- Personal communications

## Data Sharing

User data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.
