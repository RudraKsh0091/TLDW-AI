Privacy Policy for TLDW AI

Last Updated: July 2026

TLDW AI is a browser extension that enables users to ask questions about YouTube videos using AI-powered retrieval and summarization. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed and how it is used.

Information We Process

The extension may process:

  • The current YouTube video URL.
  • The video's transcript.
  • Questions entered by the user.

This information is used solely to provide AI-powered question answering, video indexing, and summarization.

Data Storage

Video transcripts may be stored on the backend to improve performance by avoiding repeated indexing of the same video.

User questions are processed to generate responses and are not used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password
  • Payment information
  • Health information
  • Location information

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for advertising, marketing, or unrelated purposes.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect processed information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
